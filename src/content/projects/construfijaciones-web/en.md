## <span id="impacto"></span>A clearer, more useful digital presence

*Commercial impact*

- Better digital presentation of the company
- More accessible information for customers and visitors
- Web structure ready for a catalog and future content
- Better communication of brands, products, and services
- A stronger foundation for commercial inquiries and positioning

## <span id="problema"></span>When the offering exists but isn't quickly understood

*Communication problem*

The company needed a clearer web presence to show who they are, what they offer, which brands they work with, and how to contact them. A poorly structured website makes it harder for a visitor to understand the offering and move toward a concrete action.

## <span id="solucion"></span>A site that guides visitors clearly

*Web structure*

Built and revamped a corporate and commercial website with a clear structure, informative sections, a catalog, brand pages, contact, and content ready to grow with the company.

## <span id="features"></span>The parts that carry the commercial message

*Elements implemented*

- Corporate home page
- Product catalog or showcase
- Clear commercial sections
- Warranty or brand information page
- About us page
- Contact page
- Responsive design
- Structure ready for future growth

## <span id="tecnico"></span>A fast, maintainable site ready to grow

*Technical decisions*

- Next.js and React to build the site, with Tailwind CSS for styling
- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for structure, styling, and interactions
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile navigation
- Optimization of visual structure and commercial content
- Organized routes and sections for quick scanning

## <span id="rol"></span>Implementation, structure, and responsiveness

*My role*

I worked on the site implementation, content structure, responsive adaptation, visual organization, commercial pages, and experience details to improve the company's navigation and digital presentation.

## <span id="ux"></span>Quick scanning without overloading the page

*Content and conversion*

The priority was making information easy to scan, with concrete sections, a clear visual hierarchy, and visible calls to action. The design aims to look professional without overwhelming the visitor with unnecessary information.

## <span id="aprendizaje"></span>Organizing the message beats adding complexity

*Web takeaway*

An effective website doesn't need to be complex: it needs to organize the message well, look trustworthy, and help people find what they're looking for. Clarity of content matters more than adding effects or unnecessary sections.
