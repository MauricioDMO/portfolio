This is my personal portfolio: a place to show what I do, the projects I work on and a way to get in touch. I also talk about Construfijaciones here and how it shows up in my work.

## What this site is

MauricioDMO is my personal brand. Here I publish case studies, my [online résumé](/en/cv/) with a downloadable PDF version, this blog and a [contact page](/en/contact/). The site is bilingual: every page has an English and a Spanish version.

My commercial brand, Silver Wolf, is a separate thing with [its own site](https://web.mauriciodmo.com). This portfolio isn't a services landing page: it's here to show the work and make professional contact easy.

## What Construfijaciones is

Construfijaciones S.A. de C.V. is the company where I work as a Software Developer, part-time since February 2024. Its [corporate website](https://construfijaciones.com) introduces the company, its products, the brands it works with and how to get in touch: a catalog, brand pages, warranty information, an About us section and contact details.

My work there is split between the commercial site and the internal systems used in day-to-day operations: electronic invoicing, inventory, warranty (RMA) management and reporting, plus process automation and user support. The figures on my résumé say around 300 DTE documents a month, more than 3,000 product codes and 50 or more RMA cases a month.
